## 简体中文

### 更新内容

- 在菜单栏图标样式下方新增「登录时自动启动」开关，默认不注册，由用户选择启用或关闭。
- 使用 macOS 原生登录项管理，状态以系统记录为准；正常退出后保留用户选择。
- 提供待系统批准说明和「管理登录项」入口，返回应用时刷新状态。更改失败会提示错误并显示实际状态。
- 同步中英文文案及使用说明。版本 `0.7.2`，构建号 `22`，保留图标及配置兼容。

### 使用与验证范围

- 请先将应用安装到 `Applications`，再开启自启动；该选项在登录 Mac 后启动应用。
- 卸载前请关闭自启动，再正常退出应用。
- 20 项登录项模拟检查、现有充电控制回归和中英文离屏布局检查通过，测试未修改本机登录项。真实登录后启动及系统批准流程尚未验收。
- DMG 在本地 Apple Silicon Mac 编译后上传；版本、图标、中英文资源、许可证、签名和 DMG 完整性已校验。

### 下载与许可证

下载 `MauLeaf-0.7.2.dmg`，先正常退出旧版，再将 `MauLeaf.app` 拖入 Applications 替换。支持 Apple Silicon（arm64），macOS 13 或更高版本；已有设置和正常工作的 batt 服务继续保留。安装包为本地 ad-hoc 签名，尚未进行 Apple notarization；首次打开可能需要在系统设置中按提示允许。可使用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 校验下载文件。

项目采用 Apache-2.0，保留原始 ChargeCap MIT 声明；内置独立组件 batt 固定为 `v0.8.0`，遵循 GPL-2.0。本页附带其对应源码 `batt-v0.8.0-source.tar.gz`；MauLeaf 源码可通过 `v0.7.2` 标签或本页源码归档获取。

## English

### What's new

- Added a “Launch at login” switch below the menu bar icon settings. No login item is registered by default; users choose whether to enable it.
- Uses native macOS login item management and reads the system registration state. Normal quit preserves the user's choice.
- Shows pending approval and provides a “Manage Login Items” shortcut. Status refreshes when the app becomes active. Failed changes show an error and the actual state.
- Updated Chinese and English text and instructions. Version `0.7.2`, build `22`, preserving icons and settings compatibility.

### Usage and validation scope

- Install the app in `Applications` before enabling this option. It starts the app after you log in to your Mac.
- Turn off launch at login before uninstalling, then quit the app normally.
- All 20 simulated login item checks, existing charging regressions, and offscreen layout checks in both languages passed. Tests did not change this Mac's login items. Actual launch after login and system approval have not been validated.
- The DMG was compiled locally on an Apple Silicon Mac before upload. Version, icons, language resources, licenses, signatures, and DMG integrity have been verified.

### Download and licenses

Download `MauLeaf-0.7.2.dmg`, quit the old app normally, then drag `MauLeaf.app` into Applications to replace it. Requires Apple Silicon (arm64), macOS 13 or later. Existing settings and a working batt service are preserved. The package is locally ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple; first launch may require approval through the prompts in System Settings. Use `SHA256SUMS.txt` to verify downloads.

The project uses Apache-2.0 and retains the original ChargeCap MIT notice. The bundled standalone batt component is pinned to `v0.8.0` and remains under GPL-2.0. Its corresponding source, `batt-v0.8.0-source.tar.gz`, is attached. MauLeaf source is available from the `v0.7.2` tag or the source archives on this page.
