## 简体中文

> **测试状态：电池供电时的叶子显示已获用户确认；「正在充电（闪电）」和「接入电源但未充电（插头）」尚未进行真机测试。**

### 更新内容

- 电量圆环中心新增供电状态图标：正在充电显示闪电，接电未充电显示插头，使用电池显示与应用图标同向、同轮廓的叶子；复用既有菜单栏品牌模板。
- 使用随深浅外观适配的柔和前景色，状态变化时短暂淡入淡出；保持圆环尺寸，不增加持续动画或采样轮询。
- 主动使用电池时优先显示叶子；低电量模式由圆环颜色及中英文状态文字区分。未知供电状态不会误显示为正在充电或使用电池。
- 版本 `0.7.4`，构建号 `26`。DMG 在本地 Apple Silicon Mac 编译后上传。

### 验证范围

- **模拟状态回归**覆盖充电、接电未充电、已充满、电池供电、低电量模式和语言切换；保留现有充电控制与双语布局回归。这些检查不代表对应状态已通过真机测试。
- 修复 SwiftUI 按名称查找独立 PNG 导致叶子空白，改为通过资源路径加载 NSImage；在真实应用包结构中检查实际圆环的明暗外观渲染、可见像素和品牌叶形。
- 三种中心图标的明暗外观已通过离屏像素渲染检查；电池供电场景的叶子显示已获用户确认。**充电中和接电未充电仍未进行真机测试**，物理插拔电源及实际充放电行为也未在本次验收。
- 已校验应用版本、图标、许可证、签名及 DMG 完整性。

### 下载与许可证

下载 `MauLeaf-0.7.4.dmg`，先正常退出旧版，再将 `MauLeaf.app` 拖入 Applications 替换。支持 Apple Silicon（arm64），macOS 13 或更高版本。安装包为本地 ad-hoc 签名，尚未进行 Apple notarization；首次打开可能需要在系统设置中按提示允许。可使用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 校验下载文件。

项目采用 Apache-2.0，保留原始 ChargeCap MIT 声明；内置独立组件 batt 固定为 `v0.8.0`，遵循 GPL-2.0。本页附带其对应源码 `batt-v0.8.0-source.tar.gz`；MauLeaf 源码可通过 `v0.7.4` 标签或本页源码归档获取。

## English

> **Test status: The leaf display while on battery power has been confirmed by the user. Charging (lightning bolt) and plugged in but not charging (plug) have not been tested on real hardware.**

### What's new

- Added power-source icons inside the battery ring: a lightning bolt while charging, a plug when connected without charging, and the app’s own leaf silhouette in its original orientation while using the battery, reusing the existing menu bar template.
- Uses a soft foreground color that adapts to light and dark appearance, with a brief fade only on state changes. Ring dimensions are preserved; no continuous animation or additional sampling is introduced.
- Active battery use takes priority and shows the leaf. Low Power Mode is distinguished by the ring color and bilingual status text. Unknown power states do not falsely show charging or battery use.
- Version `0.7.4`, build `26`. The DMG was compiled locally on an Apple Silicon Mac before upload.

### Validation scope

- **Simulated state regressions** cover charging, plugged-in idle, full battery, battery power, Low Power Mode, and language switching, alongside existing charging and bilingual layout regressions. These checks do not establish real-hardware validation of those states.
- Fixed the blank leaf caused by named SwiftUI lookup of a loose PNG. The image now loads through an explicit resource URL into NSImage. Tests render the actual ring in an app bundle and check light/dark output, visible pixels, and the brand silhouette.
- All three center icons passed offscreen pixel rendering checks in light and dark appearance. The user confirmed the leaf display on battery power. **Charging and plugged-in-but-not-charging states remain untested on real hardware.** Physical power connection changes and actual charging/discharging behavior were not validated in this update.
- App versions, icons, licenses, signatures, and DMG integrity have been verified.

### Download and licenses

Download `MauLeaf-0.7.4.dmg`, quit the old app normally, then drag `MauLeaf.app` into Applications to replace it. Requires Apple Silicon (arm64), macOS 13 or later. The package is locally ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple; first launch may require approval through the prompts in System Settings. Use `SHA256SUMS.txt` to verify downloads.

The project uses Apache-2.0 and retains the original ChargeCap MIT notice. The bundled standalone batt component is pinned to `v0.8.0` and remains under GPL-2.0. Its corresponding source, `batt-v0.8.0-source.tar.gz`, is attached. MauLeaf source is available from the `v0.7.4` tag or the source archives on this page.
