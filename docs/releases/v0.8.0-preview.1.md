## 简体中文

### MauLeaf 0.8.0-preview.1

本次预览版聚焦核心充电操作：

- 主界面仅保留当前电量、充电上限和「本次充满」。
- 充电上限支持 50–95% 调节、快捷预设和自动应用。
- 「本次充满」临时解除限充，到达 100% 后恢复原上限，也可提前取消。
- 菜单栏固定显示绿色叶子。
- 移除实时功率、主动放电、持续充满、登录项开关和图标样式选择。
- 旧版主动放电任务会在启动时尝试停止，默认策略恢复为保持适配器供电。

### 重要说明

这是功能精简预览版，不是无 batt 版本。当前自研 AppleSMC 后端已完成只读探测、策略映射、事务控制核心和辅助服务框架，但尚未在目标 M1 Pro 上完成真实 SMC 写入、固件激活及充电上限执行验证。因此本版本继续使用固定版本 batt v0.8.0 执行实际充电控制，避免在硬件验证前交付未经证实的写入路径。

目标机型验证范围：MacBookPro18,1、Apple M1 Pro、macOS 15、固件 mBoot-20457.41.3。其他机型和系统不因本次构建成功而获得原生后端兼容性声明。

### 下载

- Apple Silicon：完整功能预览版。
- Intel：未实机验证的只读实验版，不提供充电控制。

macOS 26/27 仍不受当前 MauLeaf 版本支持，请勿安装。

## English

### MauLeaf 0.8.0-preview.1

This preview focuses on the essential charging actions:

- The panel keeps only battery status, the charging limit, and one-time full charge.
- Adjust the limit from 50–95% with quick presets and automatic application.
- One-time full charge temporarily releases the limit, restores it at 100%, and can be cancelled early.
- The menu bar always displays the green leaf.
- Live power readings, active discharge, permanent full-charge mode, login-item controls, and icon-style selection are removed.
- Legacy active-discharge jobs are stopped on launch where possible; the default policy is adapter power.

### Important

This is a simplified UI preview, not a batt-free release. The in-house AppleSMC backend has read-only probing, policy mapping, a transaction core, and a helper framework, but real SMC writes, firmware activation, and charge-limit enforcement have not yet been verified on the target M1 Pro. This preview therefore continues to use pinned batt v0.8.0 for actual charging control rather than shipping an unverified hardware-write path.

Validated evidence is limited to MacBookPro18,1, Apple M1 Pro, macOS 15, and firmware mBoot-20457.41.3. A successful build does not establish compatibility with other models or operating systems.

- Apple Silicon: full-featured preview.
- Intel: untested read-only experimental build; no charging control.

macOS 26/27 remain unsupported by the current MauLeaf release.
