## 简体中文

### MauLeaf 0.8.0-preview.1 — 原生控制预览版

本次版本移除第三方充电控制后端，切换到 MauLeaf 自研 AppleSMC 控制：

- 删除 batt 二进制、第三方固件资源、调用代码、旧版后台 Agent 和第三方卸载器。
- 主界面仅保留当前电量、充电上限和「本次充满」。
- 充电上限支持 50–95% 调节和快捷预设。
- 「本次充满」通过原生固件临时关闭限充，并由 root helper 在 100%、取消或 10 小时期限后恢复。
- 菜单栏固定绿色叶子。
- 首次启动自动请求一次管理员授权，安装 MauLeaf 自己的原生 AppleSMC 服务；不再显示旧版「安装控制组件」卡片。
- Intel 版本不包含原生 helper，仅为只读实验版。

### 原生控制安全边界

写入仅允许目标机型 MacBookPro18,1、固件 mBoot-20457.41.3、macOS 15、arm64，并要求连接电源和用户明确确认。写入按 bfF0/bfD0/bfE0 事务顺序执行，逐项读回验证，失败尝试回滚。

**重要：寄存器写入和读回成功尚不代表实际充电停止点已通过实机验证。** 当前预览版需要在目标 M1 Pro 接通电源后，观察达到上限时是否停止充电、降至恢复阈值时是否重新充电。自动化测试只使用 fake transport，不写真实硬件。

macOS 26/27、Intel 和其他未验证机型不提供充电控制。

## English

### MauLeaf 0.8.0-preview.1 — Native-control preview

This release removes the third-party charging backend and switches to MauLeaf's own AppleSMC controller:

- Remove the batt executable, third-party firmware assets, invocation code, legacy background agents, and third-party uninstaller.
- Keep only battery status, charging limit, and one-time full charge in the main panel.
- Support a 50–95% limit with quick presets.
- One-time full charge temporarily disables the native firmware limit; the root helper restores it at 100%, on cancellation, or after 10 hours.
- The menu bar always displays the green leaf.
- First launch requests one administrator authorization to install MauLeaf's own AppleSMC service. The old “Install control component” card is removed.
- Intel builds remain read-only experiments without the native helper.

### Native-control safety boundary

Writes are restricted to MacBookPro18,1, firmware mBoot-20457.41.3, macOS 15, and arm64, with AC power and explicit user consent. The helper applies the bfF0/bfD0/bfE0 transaction in order, reads back each write, and attempts rollback on failure.

**Important: successful register writes and read-back do not yet prove real charging-stop behavior.** The target M1 Pro must be tested on AC power to observe whether charging stops at the upper threshold and resumes at the lower threshold. Automated tests use a fake transport and never write to physical hardware.

Charging control is unavailable on macOS 26/27, Intel, and other unverified models.
