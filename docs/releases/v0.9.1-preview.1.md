## 简体中文

### MauLeaf 0.9.1-preview.1 — 超限适配器供电观察预览

此版本在 0.9.0 原生单卡片界面上新增**设置 → 超限优先适配器**的预览开关。**这是验证阶段的只读观察功能，不是已实现的供电切换。**

#### 本次变更
- 默认关闭的独立偏好：开关保存在本机 UserDefaults，不需要管理员密码，不会改变现有限充设置。
- 当限充已启用、电量高于固件上限且检测到适配器连接时，读取电池实时电流（AppleSmartBattery / Amperage）和 IsCharging，展示当前观察结果。
- 连续至少三个 20 秒采样周期均观察到明显负向电流时，显示“连续检测到放电”，避免单次采样导致误判。充电异常、无电流读数、未插电、低于上限、本次充满等场景独立处理。
- 继续使用原本菜单栏原生卡片；设置与充电上限仍可展开，不新增滚动区或独立窗口。
- 增加纯状态策略测试、电池电流解析测试和管理器偏好/只读测试。

#### 重要安全边界
- **Preview 1 不保证也不主动实现“电量高于限充时由适配器直接供电”。** 当前仅可观察放电和充电迹象，无法依据 ExternalConnected 或接近零的电流证明实际供电路径。
- 不写入 CH0B / CH0C / CHIE 或其他新增 SMC 键；不修改 bfF0、bfD0、bfE0 的现有行为，不改变特权 Helper 的恢复、卸载和本次充满事务。
- MacBookPro18,1、macOS 15、mBoot-20457.41.3 的硬件约束不变。后续若需真正阻止超限放电，须先验证存在独立的“暂停充电且保留外接供电”能力，再设计带原始快照与失败恢复的写入流程。
- macOS 的实际限充/充放电曲线仍需真机验证。开启此选项**不会**修复超限放电问题，请勿将它作为保护电池的已生效措施。

#### 验证建议
可在 85% 以上电量、80% 限充、接电状态下开启设置，观察状态提示。若提示持续放电，记录电量与充电状态（不需要公开任何个人设备信息），继续通过 Issue 反馈。该设置只用于辅助定位。

## English

### MauLeaf 0.9.1-preview.1 — Above-limit adapter power observation

Adds an **experimental “Prefer adapter above limit”** setting to the native single-card menu bar UI. **This is read-only observation, not a working wall-power override.**

#### Changes
- Opt-in local preference, off by default; no administrator authorization or SMC write is caused by toggling it.
- Observes AppleSmartBattery `Amperage`, `IsCharging`, battery percentage and AC connection while a managed firmware charge limit is active and the battery is above that limit.
- Requires at least three negative-current samples, normally taken every 20 seconds, before reporting repeated discharge. Shows separate messages for charging above the limit, missing telemetry, no AC, below-limit level, and one-time full charge.
- Keeps the compact non-scrollable native card and adds localized text, policy tests, battery parsing tests, and manager regression coverage.

#### Safety limitations
- **This preview does not prevent battery drain above the limit or force the Mac to run from its adapter.** AC connection and near-zero battery current cannot prove which rail supplies system power.
- No new SMC keys are written; the existing bfF0/bfD0/bfE0 controller, privileged helper, full-charge flow, snapshot restoration, and exit/uninstall safety rules are unchanged.
- The validated target remains MacBookPro18,1 / macOS 15 / mBoot-20457.41.3 / arm64. Any future real power-routing control requires device-level validation and a separate recovery design.
- Do not rely on this monitoring preference as an active battery-protection mechanism.
