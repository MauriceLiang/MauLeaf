## 简体中文

### MauLeaf v0.9.1-preview.10 — 实时供电与退出组件设置

- **自动实时刷新**：参考 [Status Trio](https://github.com/lingyired/status-trio)，仅在菜单面板打开且供电详情显示时每 **2 秒**刷新功率，首次打开立即采样。关闭菜单/关闭图表则停止额外采样。移除手动「供电刷新间隔」设置。
- **实时只读 SMC**：固定读取 PDTR（适配器输入）及 B0AC/B0AV（电池功率），不可用则回退现有 AppleSmartBattery/IORegistry 遥测。每次短连接、后台 utility 队列，不重叠执行；已关闭面板的过期结果丢弃。不扫描或写入 SMC。
- **不影响固件安全**：原有 20 秒 Helper 状态检查、拔插电事件、原始固件恢复和动态保电逻辑不变。传感器可能更新慢于 2 秒，因此不能保证每次都出现新物理读数。
- **退出时移除组件**：设置中新增持久化开关，默认关闭；开启后每次退出先恢复并验证原始固件，然后删除 Helper、LaunchDaemon 和脚本；关闭则先恢复固件并保留组件，便于再次启动。
- **取消每次退出询问**：正常退出不再弹出三选一窗口，直接按照设置执行；若恢复或清理失败仍显示安全警告并阻止正常退出。
- **卸载提醒**：「建议卸载前开启，退出并确认清理完成后再删除 App。」卸载前必须验证固件恢复；不能仅靠把 App 拖到废纸篓完成后台组件卸载。
- **测试**：检查默认策略、保存后的策略、两种清理流程、2 秒采样与面板生命周期、双语文案、UI 布局和原有恢复安全回归。

**参考/授权**：Status Trio，Apache License 2.0；借鉴 [PowerSamplingService](https://github.com/lingyired/status-trio/blob/main/Sources/StatusTrioCore/Monitoring/PowerSamplingService.swift)、[SystemPowerReader](https://github.com/lingyired/status-trio/blob/main/Sources/StatusTrioCore/Monitoring/SystemPowerReader.swift) 和 [SMCReadOnlyClient](https://github.com/lingyired/status-trio/blob/main/Sources/StatusTrioCore/Monitoring/SMCReadOnlyClient.swift)，代码针对 MauLeaf 重新实现。

## English

### MauLeaf v0.9.1-preview.10 — Live Power and Saved Quit Cleanup

- Automatically update the visible Power Flow every **2 seconds**, starting immediately when the menu panel opens; stop the additional sensor reads when hidden. Remove the previous manual refresh interval control.
- Sample fixed read-only AppleSMC keys PDTR/B0AC/B0AV on a utility queue with short-lived connections and fallback to AppleSmartBattery/IORegistry data. Never use the privileged Helper for power display, issue SMC writes or display late closed-panel results.
- Keep the existing 20-second firmware safety poll, plug/unplug notifications and firmware restore logic unchanged. Physical sensor readings may update more slowly than the sampling cadence.
- Add a persistent **Remove components on quit** switch in Settings. Off (default) restores firmware and keeps the Helper for relaunch. On restores and verifies firmware first, then removes Helper, LaunchDaemon and scripts.
- Normal quit no longer asks the same question every time. Restore or cleanup failures still raise a mandatory safety warning and block normal quit.
- Before uninstalling, turn the switch On, quit and verify cleanup, then delete the App.
- Extend bilingual, UI and safety tests.

Reference: [Status Trio](https://github.com/lingyired/status-trio), Apache-2.0. The native power-sampling logic is adapted for MauLeaf.
