## 简体中文

### MauLeaf v0.9.1-preview.11 — 隐藏供电详情图时保留供电状态

- **详情图显示**：保持当前「供电详情」标题、右上角状态胶囊以及下方实时功率流向图不变。
- **详情图关闭**：顶部保留一行简洁的「供电状态」标题和原本在图表右上角的同款状态胶囊。只隐藏功率卡片及连接流带，不隐藏供电状态，不留多余空白。
- **统一状态来源**：抽离复用 MauLeafPowerStatusBadge，电池供电、适配器供电、充电中、电池辅助供电及供电状态未知五种模式均使用同一套文字、状态点颜色和辅助功能描述；中文英文都一致。
- **设置提示**：「关闭后保留供电状态，仅隐藏供电流向图」。
- **状态更新**：关闭详情图后不运行 Preview 10 的 2 秒实时图表功率采样。使用现有 IOKit 电源插拔通知及 20 秒状态检查更新简洁状态行；重新开启详情图则恢复 2 秒只读功率采样。
- **无固件变更**：不修改 SMC 固件控制、Helper、动态保电、本次充满、拔电恢复或退出时组件移除逻辑。
- **测试覆盖**：328pt 菜单内容宽度、中英文、明暗主题、五种状态文案，确保关闭图表能缩小弹窗高度，重新开启后完整图恢复，且无滚动条。

## English

### MauLeaf v0.9.1-preview.11 — Keep Power Status When the Flow Diagram Is Hidden

- When the full Power Flow diagram is enabled, keep the existing title, top-right status capsule, and live power ribbons.
- When the diagram is hidden, preserve a compact **Power** row and the identical status capsule; hide only the wattage nodes and ribbons, without a blank spacer.
- Both modes reuse **MauLeafPowerStatusBadge** for all five supply modes, colors, English/Chinese labels, and accessible status.
- Settings help now clarifies that disabling details hides only the diagram, not the power status.
- The compact status continues to follow existing IOKit power-source notifications and the 20-second safety/state fallback; the 2-second live watt sampling is inactive while details are hidden.
- No changes to firmware control, the privileged Helper, Dynamic Hold, one-time full, unplug recovery, or quit cleanup preferences.
- UI and localization checks cover five status modes in light/dark, English/Chinese and the 328pt compact menu layout.

**Scope:** Presentation-only. Power-status updates use existing system battery readings and are not a guarantee of subsecond unplug detection.
