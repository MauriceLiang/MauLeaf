## 简体中文

### MauLeaf 0.9.1-preview.4 — 动态保电实验版

适用已验证配置：**Apple Silicon arm64 / MacBookPro18,1 / macOS 15 / mBoot-20457.41.3**。此预览属于新固件写入行为，需真机验证。

- 将设置中的「超限优先适配器」替换为 **「动态保电」**，保留原生分段选择样式，默认关闭。旧只读监测偏好不会自动启用动态写入。
- **用户目标和固件实际区间分离。** 举例：目标 80%，原始电量 95.4%，临时保持上限设置为 96%（下限仍以用户目标为基准）；下次拔电再插电若电量 88.2%，重新捕获 89%；降至 80% 或更低恢复原始 80% 目标。
- 使用原始电池容量精确比例并向上取整；无法获取精确值时拒绝新的动态写入。
- 已确认物理拔电时不进行动态写入；自动重插电调整须满足连续观测与至少 8 秒稳定期，并保留 20 秒低频轮询兜底。注意系统外接状态报告可能滞后，此检测并非物理独立证明。
- 正常停用、退出、卸载、Helper 崩溃恢复和「本次充满」仍走原有安全流程；本次充满保存真实的暂时固件区间。
- Helper 升级至协议 **v6**、构建版本 **1.1.0**，支持实验性 96–100% 临时上限，并禁止旧 Helper 被误认为兼容。启动时需要 macOS 管理员授权升级已有 Helper。
- 新增纯状态规划、精度和实际 Helper 流程模拟测试。

**重要限制：** 动态保电**不等于已验证的适配器直供**。寄存器写入成功并不能保证停止充电/放电，也不能保证修复系统拔电识别延迟。若实验失败，使用原有「关闭限充」恢复固件原始状态。详见 [动态保电设计和安全边界](../DYNAMIC_HOLD.md)。

## English

### MauLeaf 0.9.1-preview.4 — Experimental Dynamic Hold

Verified firmware-write profile: **Apple Silicon arm64 / MacBookPro18,1 / macOS 15 / mBoot-20457.41.3**.

- Replaces the old read-only above-limit adapter observation switch with **Dynamic Hold** in Settings. It is opt-in and off by default; the old preference is not migrated into firmware writes.
- Keeps the user charging target independent from the temporary firmware ceiling. Above the target, use ceil of precise raw SoC (e.g. 95.4% → 96%), while keeping the user's original lower threshold. On a confirmed unplug/replug, recapture the current SoC; below target, restore the original ceiling.
- Fails closed when precise SoC is unavailable, avoids firmware writes on unplug, waits at least 8 seconds after observed reconnect before automatic updates, and retains a 20-second fallback.
- The current one-time full charge and original firmware snapshot restoration/uninstall remain authoritative.
- Privileged Helper protocol **v6**, build **1.1.0**, are required for temporary 96–100% firmware ceilings; outdated installed Helpers require an authorized upgrade.
- New policy, battery precision and manager regression tests.

**Not verified to keep the Mac powered from its adapter.** SMC readback does not prove actual power flow or that macOS disconnect reporting latency is fixed. Hardware evaluation is required. See [Dynamic Hold design and safety](../DYNAMIC_HOLD.md).
