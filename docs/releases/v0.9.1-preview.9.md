## 简体中文

### MauLeaf v0.9.1-preview.9 — 自定义供电刷新间隔与简化动态保电说明

- 在「设置 → 供电刷新间隔」中选择 **2 / 5 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 60 秒**，默认 **5 秒**。设置会持久保存，修改后立即生效。
- **仅在菜单面板打开且显示供电详情时**，按所选间隔读取只读电池功率遥测并刷新供电流向图；关闭面板或关闭图表后停止额外采样，减少后台资源占用。
- 保留原有独立的 **20 秒安全/Helper 状态检查**。用户修改供电图刷新间隔不会改变该检查频率、固件写入、恢复策略或 Helper 版本。拔插电源事件仍可立即触发状态刷新，不受上述时间间隔限制。
- 将「动态保电」说明简化为：「**超限时保持电量；关闭后拔电识别可能延迟（已测试机型）。**」工具提示同步优化；动态保电实际应用后的状态保留目标和临时上限数字，去除此前笼统的「供电效果待验证」字样。
- **实测范围**：用户报告目标 MacBookPro18,1 上启用动态保电时供电表现和拔电响应正常，而普通固定限充模式仍可能延迟。该结论仅代表已测试设备，不能推广到所有 Apple Silicon Mac；本次没有调整电源检测或恢复固件行为。
- 补充偏好持久化、定时器仅在面板打开时运行、禁用供电图后停止计时、无效间隔回退，以及中英文布局回归测试。

**安全边界：** 这次是 UI 和只读遥测周期优化，不会额外触发 Helper、管理员授权或固件控制。Helper 保持 XPC v6 / build 1.3.0，原始 SMC 快照及拔电恢复不变。供电读数可能受到系统遥测刷新周期影响；选择 2 秒不代表传感器每 2 秒一定产出新的物理采样。

## English

### MauLeaf v0.9.1-preview.9 — Configurable Power Refresh and Shorter Dynamic Hold Copy

- Settings now offer **2 / 5 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 60 seconds** for Power Flow refresh, with a **5-second default**. Selection persists and takes effect immediately.
- Additional read-only watt sampling runs only while the menu panel is open and Power Flow is visible, and stops as soon as the panel closes or the diagram is disabled.
- The existing **20-second firmware/Helper safety fallback** remains independent and unchanged. Real power-source events still refresh immediately, regardless of the chosen interval.
- Dynamic Hold help text is shorter and notes that **turning it off may delay unplug recognition on the tested Mac**. Applied Dynamic Hold state continues to show target and temporary ceiling without the obsolete blanket 'power flow unverified' suffix.
- The user has verified Dynamic Hold supply behavior and unplug response on the target Mac, but the fixed-limit case may still lag. These are device-specific observations, not a hardware-wide guarantee; this preview does not change power control.
- Tests cover persistence, invalid stored intervals, UI timer lifecycle, and bilingual localization/layout.

**Scope:** Display and read-only sampling only. No changes to privileged writes, firmware snapshots, restore safety, or Helper XPC v6/build 1.3.0. Sensor telemetry may update more slowly than the selected timer.
