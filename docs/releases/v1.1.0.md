## 简体中文

### MauLeaf 1.1.0 — 正式版

本版整合 1.0.0-fix2 至 fix5 的有效改进。应用版本 **1.1.0**，内置 Helper 版本 **1.7.0**，XPC 协议 **v9**。

- **快速退出**：应用仅等待 Helper 持久化接收恢复任务；固件恢复、读回校验与按设置保留或移除组件由后台处理。不再显示独立退出进度卡片。
- **固件初始化**：支持已验证目标设备未激活的 0/0 阈值，启用限充前保存真实原始快照，关闭限充时精确恢复。
- **断电及重新接电**：已解除限充且后台任务可靠保存时，仅显示「等待接电恢复」；重新接电主动刷新 Helper 状态，并按原有用户意图恢复限充。真实异常仍显示错误。
- **Helper 兼容性**：独立版本握手，精确匹配内置协议与构建版本；必要时安全替换不兼容 Helper，保留可信快照和恢复任务。
- **强制重置**：设置中保留恢复及孤立任务清理入口，移除重复的固件未就绪提示卡片。

#### 兼容性与安全说明

- macOS 15，Apple Silicon arm64；新固件写入仍仅限 MacBookPro18,1 / mBoot-20457.41.3 的已验证配置。
- 未激活的 0/0 初始化及接电精确恢复已通过目标设备专项实验。断电写零尚未验证，仍采用安全解除激活、接电后恢复零阈值的方式。
- 快速退出确认代表后台任务已接收，不代表固件恢复已完成。卸载前开启移除组件并确认后台清理完成；勿手动删除 Helper 或恢复快照。
- CI 采用模拟固件后端；实际充电阈值完整周期及退出耗时不作未经验证的保证。安装包采用 ad-hoc 签名，未公证。
- 从旧版升级时，内置 Helper 版本不匹配可能需要一次管理员授权。

## English

### MauLeaf 1.1.0 — Stable release

This release incorporates the fixes from 1.0.0-fix2 through fix5. App version **1.1.0**, bundled Helper **1.7.0**, XPC protocol **v9**.

- **Fast quit:** The app waits only for the Helper's durable recovery acknowledgment. Firmware restoration, verification and optional component removal run independently in the background. No separate quit progress card.
- **Firmware initialization:** Support the validated inactive zero/zero baseline; persist the measured original snapshot before enabling a limit and restore it exactly on disable.
- **Unplug and reconnect:** Confirmed inactive deferred recovery appears as a normal waiting status, not a duplicate warning. Refresh Helper state on AC reconnect and resume the previously requested limit when safe.
- **Helper compatibility:** Independent version handshake, exact version matching and safe replacement of incompatible Helpers while preserving recovery records.
- **Force Reset:** Retain recovery and orphan-task cleanup in Settings without a redundant firmware-readiness card.

#### Compatibility and safety

- macOS 15, Apple Silicon arm64. New firmware writes remain restricted to the validated MacBookPro18,1 / mBoot-20457.41.3 configuration.
- Inactive zero-band initialization and exact restoration on AC were verified on hardware. Offline zero writes remain unverified; deactivation and AC-deferred restoration retain their safety boundaries.
- A fast-quit acknowledgment confirms ownership transfer, not completed hardware recovery. Verify background cleanup before uninstalling; do not manually delete the Helper or recovery records.
- CI uses simulated firmware. Full physical charge cycles and actual quit latency are not guaranteed by CI. The installer is ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
- Upgrading from an incompatible Helper may require one administrator authorization.
